02

How it works

Four steps, and you are only asked to do something in the first one.

  1. 01

    Tell us what you need

    A sentence is enough to start. No technical vocabulary required, and no form with twenty mandatory fields.

  2. 02

    We scope it and quote it

    You get a written scope: what is included, what is not, how long it takes and what it costs. In writing, before anything begins.

  3. 03

    Our team builds it

    The work goes to the people in our studio who do that discipline every day. We brief them, so you are not explaining your idea twice.

  4. 04

    Delivered, and handed over properly

    You get the finished work, the files, the access and a walkthrough. You own all of it. Nothing is held hostage.

03

Why work with us

Four things we put in writing on every project, so you know what you are getting before you commit to anything.

The quote is the price

Scope is agreed in writing before work starts. If you later want something that is not in it, we price that separately and you decide. The number does not quietly climb.

You talk to one person

You are not passed between a salesperson, an account manager and a developer. One contact who knows your project and answers in plain English.

We say no when it is not our work

If a project needs something outside what our team does well, we will tell you that instead of taking the job and learning on your budget.

You own the output

Code, design files, domains and accounts are all in your name and handed over at the end. No dependency on us to make a text change.
